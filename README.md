### 📊 6 Visualizations I Built

**1. 🌡️ Temperature Trend (1880-2024)**
- Line chart - Global avg temperature rise +1.2°C
- Shows hottest years: 2023, 2024

**2. 🏭 CO2 Ranking by Country**
- Bar chart - Top emitters: China, USA, India
- 2023 data

**3. 🌊 Sea Level Rise**
- Area chart - 3.4mm/year rise
- 1993-2024 data

**4. 🌲 Forest Area Loss**
- Donut/Bar chart - Forest area decreasing 5% per decade
- Amazon & Global forest data

**5. ⚡ Renewable vs CO2**
- Combo chart - Renewable energy growth vs CO2 emissions
- Shows renewable increasing but CO2 still high

**6. ⚠️ Extreme Events**
- Column/Map chart - Floods, Wildfires, Heatwaves count by year
- 2000-2024 increase 3x

### 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Data Cleaning:** Excel / Google Sheets
- **Dashboard:** Power BI / Tableau / Excel
- **Dataset:** NASA, NOAA, Our World in Data, Global Forest Watch
- **No Python - Pure DVT Project**

### 📈 Main Insights
- Temperature +1.2°C in 100 years
- CO2 still increasing despite renewable growth
- Forest area main reason for carbon storage loss
- Extreme events tripled in last 20 years
